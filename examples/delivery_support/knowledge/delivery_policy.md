# Marking orders delivered

A courier can mark an order delivered only inside the geofence around the delivery pin. When the
customer moves the drop-off after ordering, the courier is often outside the geofence at the new
address. Support may mark the order delivered at the new location once the customer confirms the
change, either in the order chat or on a call with support.

Never mark an order delivered on the courier's word alone. If the customer cannot be reached or
does not confirm, offer the courier a handoff to the escalations team.

# Refunds

Support can refund up to 25 dollars once per call. Anything larger goes to a human.
